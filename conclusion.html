为什么只有几行选择条件，也能产生收益？Why can a few routing lines produce a gain?

PA（Paged Attention，分页注意力）和 FIA（Fused Infer Attention，融合推理注意力）在这里是两个已有注意力实现。两者都能使用分页键值缓存；并不是只有 PA 支持分页。我们改的是选择哪个实现，没有重写内核或模型权重。PA (Paged Attention) and FIA (Fused Infer Attention) are two existing attention implementations here. Both can consume paged key/value caches; paging is not exclusive to PA. The patch changes which implementation is selected, without rewriting the kernels or weights.

可以替换的边界：单查询标准解码、相同查询与缓存、页表、有效长度、缩放和输出语义。滑动窗口、任意掩码、多查询、量化缓存或需要额外输出统计时，不能直接推广这个替换。浮点归约不同可能改变后续生成；数学任务相同不代表输出逐位相同。Substitution requires standard single-query decoding with matched query/cache data, block tables, valid lengths, scale, and output semantics. Do not generalize it directly to sliding windows, arbitrary masks, multiple queries, quantized caches, or consumers of extra output statistics. Floating-point reductions can affect subsequent generation; matching mathematics does not mean bitwise-identical output.
